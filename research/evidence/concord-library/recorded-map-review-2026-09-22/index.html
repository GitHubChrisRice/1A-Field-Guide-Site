    Concord Library — Recorded Map Review¶

    Facility: Concord Library
    Address: 2900 Salvio Street, Concord, California 94519
    Review date: 2026-09-22
    Facility Prep chain: assessor Book 111 Page 24 → Recorded Maps APN 111 24 → 7LSM51

    Source resolution¶

    Facility Prep queried Contra Costa Public Works' public Laserfiche Recorded Maps search.

    An APN-metadata search for 111 24 returned one recorded-map entry:

    • name: 7LSM51
    • entry ID: 126622
    • record type: Record of Survey Map
    • recorded-map book/page: 7 / 51
    • date recorded: 1940-09-24
    • engineer-surveyor: C. W. Gibbs
    • road vicinity: Willow Pass
    • APN metadata: 111 24
    • record ID: 51269

    The ordinary electronic-file route reports that entry 126622 has no electronic file, but the public WebLink viewer identifies one scanned page. Facility Prep used the ordinary anonymous page/tile renderer and preserved a 3600 × 2738 PNG assembled from that public page.

    Preserved render SHA-256:

    9a5198f3b9d6af2a9e00fb9b2ac8947a1902df6d23c068df5a7f1299df4a2b19

    No authentication, CAPTCHA bypass, access-control bypass, or paid transaction was used.

    Human-reviewed content¶

    The recorded survey is titled/described on the face as a survey of:

    A portion of the property of the Concord Home Acres Company

    The sheet states that the deed for that property was recorded in Book 454 of Official Records, Page 154, Contra Costa County, and that the property was located in Rancho Monte del Diablo.

    The survey further states that the course of Salvio Street, as extended, was taken as the basis for bearings. The drawing depicts the centerline/course of Salvio Street as extended, Southern Pacific Northern Railway, Willow Pass Road, iron bars/pipes, bearings/distances, and a surveyed area marked approximately 10.60 acres.

    The survey was approved September 23, 1940 and filed September 24, 1940.

    Followed record reference¶

    Facility Prep submitted the exact known reference Book 454 / Page 154 to the County Recorder's public RecorderWorks Old Book lookup.

    The public index returned:

    • document ID: 12286714
    • viewer result: Images may not be viewed online

    Automation stopped at that point. The underlying instrument is therefore a manual-copy target, not an online-retrieved source.

    The two assessor-sheet Official Records references were also resolved to public RecorderWorks document identifiers:

    • 3356 OR 502 → document ID 16109166
    • 636 OR 390 → document ID 12566187

    For both, the public viewer likewise states that images may not be viewed online.

    Evidentiary significance¶

    7LSM51 is useful because it independently ties APN book/page 111 24 to a recorded 1940 survey and to an older deed chain, and it confirms that Salvio Street was already a survey-bearing reference in that historical tract.

    It does not establish by itself:

    • the current surveyed boundary of APN 111-240-006 or 111-240-014;
    • the current edge of Salvio Street or Parkside Drive public right-of-way;
    • current easements or dedications;
    • current front/rear parking-lot control;
    • the current City/County operating allocation;
    • constitutional forum status.

    The present public-record chain has therefore reached a legitimate manual-acquisition boundary for the three Official Records instruments above.

    Current research disposition¶

    For purposes of the Concord exterior forum analysis, exact deed retrieval is not a prerequisite to applying Prigmore provisionally where the outcome does not turn on the precise line between two parcels that current official sources already identify as City-owned and publicly used.

    The exact ROW/property line remains material when distinguishing an ordinary municipal sidewalk from a facility-specific approach or when a removal/trespass question turns on a few feet of boundary. Those issues remain separately flagged.
