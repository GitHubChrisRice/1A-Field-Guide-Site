    Concord Library — City Engineering / ROW Records Target¶

    Facility: Concord Library, 2900 Salvio Street, Concord, CA 94519
    Prepared: 2026-09-22
    Target agency: City of Concord Engineering Division / City Clerk public-records process
    Status: READY-FOR-MANUAL-INQUIRY-OR-CPRA

    Why this request is now appropriate¶

    Facility Prep has already resolved:

    • Library APN 111-240-006;
    • adjoining Civic Center APN 111-240-014;
    • current City GIS owner fields;
    • Assessor Book 111 Page 24;
    • City Road Centerline segments for Salvio Street and Parkside Drive;
    • historical Record of Survey 7LSM51;
    • known Official Records references and RecorderWorks document identifiers;
    • Public Works Recorded Maps searches by APN metadata and Road Vicinity.

    Those automated/public-index paths did not produce a clearly relevant modern map fixing the present legal ROW edge at the Library/Civic Center frontage. The remaining request can therefore be narrow and record-specific rather than speculative.

    Official City routes¶

    Engineering Division:

    https://www.cityofconcord.org/271/Engineering-Division

    The City describes Engineering as providing public assistance with street inquiries and publishes the Current Development / Permit Center contact at 1950 Parkside Drive, MS/51.

    Permit-record information:

    https://www.cityofconcord.org/187/Permit-Records-Requests

    The City states that Engineering permit history can be searched through the Permit Portal and that permit documents/drawings may be reviewed through the Permit Center. Some professional drawings may have additional copy restrictions.

    General public-records route:

    https://www.cityofconcord.org/1234/Request

    The City provides an Open Public Records request route. City FAQ material also states that public records may be requested from the City Clerk or the City department holding the records.

    Preferred initial request¶

    Before making a broad request, ask Engineering whether it can identify the controlling current map/plan/document numbers:

    I am researching the present property/right-of-way configuration around Concord Library and the Concord Civic Center. Please identify any current right-of-way map, record of survey, street dedication/acceptance map, engineering plan, parcel/tract map, or other official record showing the present public right-of-way edges of Salvio Street and Parkside Drive adjacent to APNs 111-240-006 and 111-240-014. Please also identify any dedication, easement, right-of-entry, or other recorded document referenced by those plans that affects the sidewalks, driveways, entrance approaches, or parking/circulation areas immediately adjoining those parcels. If no single current map exists, please identify the controlling plan/map/document numbers used by the City to determine those ROW edges.

    Narrow CPRA version if needed¶

    If Engineering cannot answer informally, request identifiable existing records rather than asking the City to create an analysis:

    Please provide existing City records sufficient to show the present public right-of-way boundaries for Salvio Street and Parkside Drive adjacent to APNs 111-240-006 and 111-240-014, including any right-of-way maps, street dedication or acceptance records, records of survey, parcel/tract maps, engineering plans, legal descriptions, easement documents, or index/list of controlling map or plan numbers maintained by the City. Please also provide any existing City record identifying which agency or department controls or maintains the sidewalks, driveways, entrance approaches, and public parking/circulation areas immediately adjoining Concord Library at 2900 Salvio Street and the Civic Center parcel at 1950 Parkside Drive. Electronic copies are preferred where available.

    Separate operational-control target¶

    If the boundary record does not answer control allocation, a separate request should seek:

    Any current agreement, lease, license, memorandum of understanding, maintenance agreement, parking agreement, joint-use agreement, or similar operative document between the City of Concord and Contra Costa County / Contra Costa County Library concerning Concord Library at 2900 Salvio Street, including exterior parking, sidewalks/approaches, security, maintenance, or shared Civic Center circulation.

    Keeping this second request separate makes it easier to distinguish property/ROW evidence from operational-control evidence.

    Evidence to supply if staff asks for context¶

    Use these identifiers:

    • facility: Concord Library, 2900 Salvio Street;
    • Library APN: 111-240-006;
    • Civic Center APN: 111-240-014;
    • assessor sheet: Book 111 Page 24;
    • current Salvio Road Centerline OBJECTID: 199013;
    • current Parkside Road Centerline OBJECTID: 199079;
    • historical Record of Survey: 7LSM51, County Public Works entry 126622;
    • assessor notation for Library parcel: 3356 OR 502, RecorderWorks docid 16109166;
    • assessor notation for Civic Center parcel: 636 OR 390, RecorderWorks docid 12566187.

    Review rule¶

    A City response identifying a map or plan should be preserved and reviewed as evidence. Do not automatically convert a staff email, permit drawing, or engineering plan into a constitutional classification. The project still must identify the exact area and then apply forum doctrine independently.
