Map-hardening research tasks

These tasks improve exact geometry or source support. They do not erase classifications already supported by the current record.

Boundary evidence

Confirm sidewalk and plaza right-of-way geometry

Locate the controlling City parcel/ROW/easement map or recorded dedication showing the exact Broadway and Lincoln sidewalk edges and the civic-plaza transition.

Site plan

Locate final as-built/site circulation plan

Find an official plan showing the library footprint, plaza, surface parking, underground garage access, Civic Park connections, and pedestrian circulation.

Agency position

Find any CCCL/City forum or solicitation guidance

Locate any formal legal memo, enforcement guidance, permit rule, training, or correspondence that states how the City or County classifies exterior areas or applies solicitation/leafleting restrictions there.
