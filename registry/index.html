    Facility Registry Preview¶

    Working preview. The Registry turns the Field Guide's legal research into place-specific preparation notes. It does not assign one blanket label to an entire building. Each facility is broken into the areas, activities, rules, and unresolved facts that can actually change the analysis.

    Verified documentary fact confirmed from reviewed sources Strongly supported substantial factual/doctrinal support, with stated limits Unresolved evidence is not sufficient for a confident conclusion; not a prohibition Field check pending current signage, layout, access, or other physical fact still needs verification

    Current pilot facilities¶

    Concord Library¶

    2900 Salvio Street, Concord
    Municipal public library · Contra Costa County Library

    Field check pending Facility overall: unresolved

    What the research currently supports

    • City ownership of the Library parcel and adjoining Civic Center parcel is strongly supported.
    • Exterior entry/open-space, pedestrian, and parking questions have substantial public-forum support under area-specific authority; exact edges remain relevant where removal/trespass turns on location.
    • The meeting-room channel is separately analyzed and strongly supports limited-public-forum treatment under Faith Center.
    • Ordinary interior recording/forum doctrine remains unresolved.
    • No dedicated public-facing Concord-specific ordinary-patron photography/videography rule was located in the online sources reviewed.

    Highest-value open facts: current signs, physical entrance/parking configuration, precise exterior control where outcome-determinative, and ordinary-interior recording treatment.

    Concord DMV Field Office¶

    2070 Diamond Boulevard, Concord
    California Department of Motor Vehicles

    Federal lobby: nonpublic strongly supported California Speech Clause: unresolved Access path / field check pending

    What the research currently supports

    • State ownership and DMV operation are strongly supported for APN 126-490-004.
    • The ordinary customer lobby/waiting channel is strongly supported as a federal nonpublic forum under the administrative-interior authorities reviewed.
    • California Speech Clause treatment of that lobby remains unresolved.
    • The knowledge-test phone/device rule is activity-specific and is not treated as a field-office-wide camera rule.
    • No dedicated public-facing California DMV field-office ordinary-patron photography/videography rule was located in the online sources reviewed.
    • The Diamond Boulevard address does not itself prove direct parcel frontage: an intervening parcel creates an unresolved modern access-path question.

    Highest-value open facts: the actual Diamond ingress route/legal interest, current parking/queue geometry, entrance signs, and physical subarea boundaries.

    Concord Police Headquarters¶

    1350 Galindo Street, Concord
    City of Concord Police Department

    Federal lobby: purpose-limited treatment strongly supported California Speech Clause: unresolved Lobby/signage field check pending

    What the research currently supports

    • City ownership and direct Galindo frontage are strongly supported; no DMV-like address/frontage mismatch was found.
    • The ordinary public lobby / Community Service Desk is a purpose-limited public-service interior. The federal limited/nonpublic restriction standard is strongly supported; California classification remains unresolved.
    • CPD Policy 423 affirmatively recognizes lawful public recording of department members performing official duties.
    • Penal Code § 148(g) separately protects recording an officer, by itself, from becoming § 148(a) obstruction or the sole basis for reasonable suspicion/probable cause when its location conditions are met.
    • Wilson v. County of Contra Costa is a close local, nonbinding sheriff-lobby recording comparator, with important pleading-stage limits.
    • No separate published Concord Police Headquarters lobby-wide ordinary-patron camera prohibition was located in the online sources reviewed.

    Highest-value open facts: lobby entrance/signage, counter/privacy configuration, controlled-door boundaries, restrooms/public-area limits, and parking/pedestrian approach.

    How to read a Registry entry¶

    A Registry entry is not a green-light/red-light score for an entire address. The project keeps these layers separate:

    exact area → public access → ownership/control → forum classification → specific activity → actual restriction → activity-specific authority → removal/trespass/enforcement authority

    That distinction is intentional. A facility can contain an ordinary municipal sidewalk, public lobby, transaction counter, meeting room, employee corridor, secure records area, and parking facility under materially different rules.

    What “unresolved” means¶

    Unresolved means the project does not yet have enough authority or current facts to make the narrower conclusion confidently. It does not mean prohibited.

    Likewise, “nonpublic forum” does not mean “no First Amendment rights.” It identifies the constitutional standard governing an actual restriction; it does not manufacture a rule the government has not adopted.
