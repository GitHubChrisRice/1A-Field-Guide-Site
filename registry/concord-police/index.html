    City of Concord Police Department · CA-013-F0004

    Bottom line: The ordinary public lobby / Community Service Desk and Records counter are NPF/HIGH federally; the ordinary Galindo municipal sidewalk is TPF/HIGH; the Community Meeting Room is LPF/HIGH.     Recording posture¶

    CPD Policy 423 recognizes lawful public recording of department members performing official duties. Penal Code § 148(g) separately protects recording-alone within its location conditions. No separate published lobby-wide ordinary-patron camera prohibition was located online.

    • Current entrance/lobby signs
    • Counter privacy configuration
    • Controlled-door boundaries
    • Public-restroom limits
    • Exact front parking/pedestrian approach footprint

    What remains legally unresolved¶

    • Any actual lobby recording restriction not located online
    • Privacy/confidentiality analysis for victim/witness/private-report subjects
