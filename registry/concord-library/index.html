    Concord Library¶

    2900 Salvio Street, Concord
    Contra Costa County Library · City-owned library parcel
    Registry hardening: September 30, 2026 · Post-activation rerun / pre-field

    Bottom line: Most of the useful forum map can be classified before a field visit. The ordinary municipal sidewalks, entrance/approach, Civic Center walkway, and front public lot are TPF / HIGH. The ordinary staffed interior is LPF / MEDIUM for library-purpose activity, while the meeting room is LPF / HIGH. Recording inside remains a separate activity question.

    Post-activation rerun: This facility was rechecked against the Active California overlay on September 30, 2026. Remaining California UNRESOLVED labels are facility-specific state-law, local-fact, or field-verification questions—not statewide qualification gaps. See the pilot rerun record.

    Recording posture¶

    No dedicated public-facing Concord-specific ordinary-patron photography/videography prohibition was located in the online sources reviewed.

    That does not equal a categorical right to record every subject inside. In the LPF interior, a restriction must still be tied to the library's purpose, actual policy, privacy, disruption, safety, or another valid basis. The Registry therefore does not convert “no ban located” into “everything may be filmed.”

    What a field visit still adds¶

    A field visit is useful for current signs, exact entrance/forecourt geometry, the rear-lot reserved areas, and any ambiguous ROW transition. None of those facts presently prevents classification of the core sidewalk, entrance, front-lot, interior-library-purpose, or meeting-room channels.
