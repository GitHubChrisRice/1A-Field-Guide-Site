## Recording posture

**No dedicated public-facing California DMV field-office ordinary-patron photography/videography prohibition was located in the online sources reviewed.**

That is not the same as a judicially established right to film throughout the lobby. The federal NPF classification tells us the standard for an actual restriction; it does not create one. The knowledge-test device rule is **testing-specific** and must not be generalized into a branch-wide camera rule.

## What a field visit still adds

The most important unresolved fact is the actual Diamond ingress path and its legal/control geometry. Current parking, queueing, entrance signs and kiosk placement also remain useful. None of that prevents the federal NPF classification of the ordinary customer-service interior.
