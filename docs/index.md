# 1A Field Guide

**Working public research preview.** The 1A Field Guide is a nationwide, Constitution-first framework for researching public photography, recording, access, government restrictions, police encounters, removal/trespass, public records, and facility-specific enforcement.

The project asks a practical question:

> **What legal rule actually applies to this activity, in this exact place, under this specific government response?**

## Nationwide status

The public shell now tracks **Federal, all 50 states, and the District of Columbia**.

- **1 Active**: California
- **1 Field Guide Development:** Federal
- **50 Not yet qualified**

Browse the [nationwide jurisdiction index](jurisdictions/index.md).

California became the project's first **Active** jurisdiction on September 30, 2026 after completing the formal qualification matrix, adversarial review, Founder/bootstrap provenance control, and separate human activation decision.

## Constitution First

> **A posted rule is a fact. Its constitutionality is a legal conclusion. Never confuse the two.**

Every material restriction is tested through:

`protected activity → exact place/forum → claimed restriction → source of authority → constitutional test → activity-specific law → enforcement authority`

Read the governing [Constitution First](constitution-first.md) method.

## Facility Registry preview

The [Facility Registry](registry/index.md) applies the methodology to specific public facilities. California's Active statewide framework now governs facility research. The four Contra Costa pilot facilities have now been reconciled against the Active California framework, moving California facility research maturity to **Researching** while the other counties remain Unresearched.

The Registry deliberately uses **UNRESOLVED** when the evidence does not justify a confident conclusion. Unresolved does not mean prohibited.

## California active handbook

The [California handbook](california/README.md) is the first Active state overlay and nationwide reference implementation. It activated with 69 Supported, 4 Researched-Unresolved, and 0 Gap rows; facility research is now **Researching** through the reconciled Contra Costa pilot set, with a complete 58-county coverage inventory.

## Federal premises

Federal premises use a separate applicability track. A federal property located inside a state does not automatically inherit that state's conduct, public-records, or facility rules merely because of geography.

Current development material includes [Federal FOIA](federal/foia.md) and [USPS](federal/usps.md).

## Research standard

Every substantive rule should be traceable to authority a reader can independently verify. The project distinguishes controlling authority, persuasive authority, policy, observed conduct, allegations, evidence, and project conclusions.

A jurisdiction being **Not yet qualified** does not mean ordinary constitutional protections disappear. A jurisdiction being **Active** means only that the Coalition's analytical framework has passed its own qualification gate.

## Working-draft notice

This project is not legal advice. Statutes, regulations, policies, and case law change, and real encounters can turn on facts not present in a simplified scenario. Check verification dates and primary sources before relying on a rule.
